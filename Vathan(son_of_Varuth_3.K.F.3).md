## VATHAN

3.K.F.4/G6.**VATHAN**3.T.F.3. Vathan's (3.K.F.4) old Tharavad house was situated in front of the new St. Andrew's Church, near the northern side of the pond. This Tharavad and property were exchanged with the church property near, and western side of the St. George Church. this old Tharavad became the old "Anchel/Post office" for about nearly Fifty years it was situated there.
